Our review checklist

Use these points consistently when researching any service. Do not claim a feature or price unless you have verified it from the provider.

01 / COST

Pricing & billing

List the current price, billing frequency, renewal terms, trial conditions and cancellation method.

02 / PRIVACY

Privacy & security

Check the privacy policy, account controls, age checks, security options and data deletion process.

03 / TERMS

Rules & support

Review geographic availability, platform rules, customer support and refund procedures.
